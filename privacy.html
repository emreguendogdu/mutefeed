Privacy Policy

Updated October 10, 2026

Mutefeed doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your settings stay on your device.

What the extension reads

When you open YouTube, Instagram, X, Facebook, Reddit or LinkedIn in Safari, the extension reads the page so it can find the parts you've switched off and hide them. This happens on your device. Nothing from the page is saved or sent anywhere.

The extension only runs on those six sites, and only where you've allowed it in Safari's settings.

Your settings

Your switches are saved on your device, and each device keeps its own. There's nothing to sign in to.

Hide rules

Sites change their layouts often. To keep up, the Mutefeed app downloads a public rules file from this website. Everyone gets the same file, and the request doesn't include an account, a device ID or anything about you or your browsing.

This website

This site doesn't set cookies or load anything from other services. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and like most web hosts, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. I don't have access to those logs.

Purchases

Apple handles the purchase. I never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Changes

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.

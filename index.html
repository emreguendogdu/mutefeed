07 / 07Setup

Made for Safari.

Mutefeed works on the sites you open in Safari. Apple keeps apps separate, so the YouTube and Instagram apps stay as they are. Open the sites in Safari for the clean version.

iPhone and iPad

  1. 01Open Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions.
  2. 02Turn on Mutefeed and allow it on the sites you use.
  3. 03Open youtube.com in Safari, tap the Mutefeed icon and pick what to hide.

Mac

  1. 01Open the Mutefeed app once.
  2. 02In Safari, choose Settings > Extensions and tick Mutefeed.
  3. 03Open youtube.com, click the Mutefeed icon and pick what to hide.

Turn it on

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions. On iOS 17 and earlier it's Settings > Safari > Extensions.
  2. Tap Mutefeed and turn it on.
  3. Under Permissions, set the sites you use (YouTube, Instagram and so on) to Allow.
  4. Open youtube.com in Safari, tap the page menu or puzzle-piece icon in the address bar, then tap Mutefeed to pick what to hide.

Mac

  1. Open the Mutefeed app once.
  2. In Safari, choose Safari > Settings > Extensions.
  3. Tick Mutefeed.
  4. Open youtube.com, click the Mutefeed icon in the toolbar and allow it on the site when Safari asks. Then pick what to hide.

Questions

Why doesn't it work in the Instagram or YouTube app?

Mutefeed is a Safari extension, so it works on websites you open in Safari. Apple doesn't let one app change how another app looks, so the Instagram, YouTube, X, Facebook, Reddit and LinkedIn apps stay as they are. Open those sites in Safari to get the cleaned-up version. If you keep opening the app out of habit, deleting it helps.

Something I switched off is showing again

Sites change their layouts often. Mutefeed downloads fixed hide rules on its own, so most fixes reach you without an app update. If something still shows:

  1. Reload the page.
  2. Check Mutefeed is still allowed on that site (see the steps above).
  3. Still there? Email me the site and a screenshot.

How do I see the whole site for a minute?

Each site has a Pause switch. Turn it on and that site shows everything until you turn it off again.

Where are my settings saved?

On your device. Each device keeps its own switches, so set them once on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Do I pay again for my iPad or Mac?

One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac. Download it on each device with the same Apple ID.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles every payment, so refunds go through Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find Mutefeed and choose to request a refund.
